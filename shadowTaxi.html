Shadow Taxi

Shadow Taxi is an arcade-style driving game where players take on the role of a taxi driver navigating busy streets to pick up and drop off passengers. The goal is to deliver passengers as close to their destination as possible to maximize points, while avoiding traffic and managing vehicle damage. With fast-paced movement, risk-reward scoring, and collectible power-ups, the game challenges players to balance precision driving with quick decision-making.

Secure dispatch. Clear trip visibility.

Context

Why it was built and constraints that shape the build.

Shadow Taxi was a project developed for fun, with a focus on gameplay mechanics, player feedback, and moment-to-moment challenge. The core loop centers around picking up one passenger at a time and transporting them safely to their destination. Players are rewarded based on how accurately they deliver passengers—dropping them closer to the target location results in higher scores, encouraging careful navigation rather than reckless driving.

The game environment is filled with other vehicles that the player must dodge. Collisions cause damage to the taxi, and once enough damage is accumulated, the vehicle is destroyed, ending the run. This damage system adds tension and forces players to balance speed with caution.

To support varied gameplay and player strategy, Shadow Taxi includes multiple power-ups that can temporarily protect the taxi or make navigation easier. These power-ups create moments of relief and momentum, helping players recover from mistakes or push for higher scores.

Overall, the project allowed me to experiment with scoring systems, player risk–reward mechanics, and game feel, while building a complete, replayable experience from concept to implementation.

Architecture

Service boundaries built for dependable operations.

Java
  • Modular Java classes separating gameplay logic, rendering, and input handling.
  • Central game loop managing state updates, collisions, and scoring.
  • Entity-based system for taxis, traffic vehicles, passengers, and power-ups.
