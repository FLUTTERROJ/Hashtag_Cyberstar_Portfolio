Support Atlas

This project is a web-based mental health support platform designed to make professional help more accessible.

Find mental-health support, fast.

Problem & Context

Why it mattered and constraints that shape the build.

Finding mental health support can be overwhelming, especially during moments of distress. Existing directories are often too broad or disconnected, delaying access when users need immediate help.

I scoped the build to solve one major bottleneck: connecting users quickly with relevant support based on location and need.

A key limitation was the private nature of health data and service boundaries. Integration into live systems required careful privacy design.

Balancing speed, trust, and clarity drove the architecture and product choices throughout implementation.
