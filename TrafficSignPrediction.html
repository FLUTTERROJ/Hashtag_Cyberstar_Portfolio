Traffic Sign Prediction

A computer vision project to classify traffic signs with high precision under noisy, real-world conditions.

Fast sign classification for safer autonomous perception.

Problem & Context

Road-sign recognition must be fast, robust, and consistent.

Traffic scenes include blur, occlusion, and lighting variance, which can reduce model reliability in deployment.

The objective was to build a lean predictor that remains stable across common environmental distortions.

Constraints included limited training time and balancing inference speed against classification accuracy.

The project centered on practical performance, not only benchmark scores.
